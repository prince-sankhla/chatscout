# ChatScout Search-Demand Inventory

Research snapshot: 2 September 2026.

This inventory intentionally separates **search demand** from **publishable supply**. A keyword is not promoted to an indexable landing page unless ChatScout currently has at least three published communities on a sensible platform/category mapping. Demand-only opportunities remain backlog items until enough legitimate supply exists.

## Priority model

- **Tier 1:** very high demand or unusually strong demand + supply fit.
- **Tier 2:** high demand / high-intent opportunities with solid supply or a clear path to supply.
- **Tier 3:** medium or unverified-volume demand with useful existing supply.
- **Tier 4:** high-intent niche/long-tail opportunities worth testing after core supply grows.

Search volume is recorded only when a public third-party snapshot exposed a figure. `Unavailable` means no reliable public figure was found during this research pass; it is not a zero-volume claim.

## Supply leaders

The strongest current supply-to-demand fits are study on Telegram (35), coding on Telegram (28), jobs on Telegram (26), coding on Discord (20), gaming on Discord (15), and student Instagram group chats via the College Students taxonomy (11). These are the safest places to build SEO landing pages now because the page can immediately satisfy the join intent with multiple real listings.

## Demand with insufficient supply

The most important supply gaps are Tamil WhatsApp groups, news WhatsApp groups, YouTube/promotion WhatsApp groups, stock-market WhatsApp groups, IAS/education WhatsApp groups, and dedicated language-based Telegram pages. Competitor SEO snapshots show clear search demand for several of these terms, but the current ChatScout inventory does not have enough trustworthy mappings to publish a useful dedicated page yet.

## Data-quality findings

As of the research pass, ChatScout has **330 published communities**: Discord 64, Instagram 98, Telegram 154, and WhatsApp 14. Published verification status is still mostly unverified: Discord 64, Instagram 90 unverified + 8 verified, Telegram 154, WhatsApp 14. Source URLs are missing for all 64 Discord listings, 93 Instagram listings, all 154 Telegram listings, and all 14 WhatsApp listings. Member counts are missing on 5 Discord, 17 Instagram and 11 WhatsApp listings; Telegram member counts are present for the current inventory.

The main practical implication is that the next data-collection push should prioritize WhatsApp supply and add source URLs to high-traffic/high-intent listings before scaling SEO pages further.

## Implementation notes

Implemented demand hubs are server-rendered Next.js routes using the existing Supabase community and category tables. They reuse the existing community cards, taxonomy links, canonical metadata conventions, and brand/UI styling. The sitemap now includes only configured demand pages whose current platform/category supply is at least three published communities, plus their collection hubs. This avoids generating hundreds of thin keyword pages.
